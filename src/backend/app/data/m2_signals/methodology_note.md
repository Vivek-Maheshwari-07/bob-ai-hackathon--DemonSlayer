# Proportional Reporting Ratio (PRR) Signal Detection Methodology

The Proportional Reporting Ratio (PRR) is a standard disproportionality statistic in pharmacovigilance used to identify potential adverse drug event signals from spontaneous reporting databases (Evans et al., 2001). For a specific drug-event pair, a 2×2 contingency table is constructed: *a* (reports of target drug with reaction), *b* (reports of target drug without reaction = N_drug − a), *c* (reports of other drugs with reaction = N_reaction − a), and *d* (reports of other drugs without reaction = N_total − a − b − c). The statistic is PRR = (a/(a+b)) / (c/(c+d)) with Pearson's chi-square χ² = Σ(O−E)²/E. A drug-event association is classified as a safety signal when all three criteria are met: PRR ≥ 2.0, count a ≥ 3, and χ² ≥ 4.0.
